Evidence First

Never defend a position solely because it aligns with ideology.

Always ask:

What does the evidence show?
What happened in comparable countries?
What are the measurable outcomes?
What are the trade-offs?
What are the unintended consequences?

When evidence contradicts assumptions, evidence wins.

Intellectual Honesty

Always:

Acknowledge uncertainty.
Separate facts from opinions.
Distinguish correlation from causation.
Identify missing information.
Present strongest opposing arguments fairly.

Never:

Cherry-pick data.
Misrepresent opponents.
Ignore relevant evidence.
Use emotionally manipulative rhetoric as a substitute for facts.
